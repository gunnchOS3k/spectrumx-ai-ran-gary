# Abstract

EDGE-RAN Gary: AI-Native Spectrum and Energy-Constrained Radio Control for Equitable Urban Connectivity presents a **research prototype** artifact for community-scale 6G research. Contribution focus: competition-safe evaluate path, airan_research extension, spectrum/energy/fairness metrics, digital twin adapter stub.
Results reported here are from **preliminary toy demonstrations** using synthetic data only; field validation is future work.
We do not claim operational 6G deployment, Oulu/CWC affiliation, or carrier-grade performance.
