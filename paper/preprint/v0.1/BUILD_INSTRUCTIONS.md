Build from this directory: `sh BUILD.sh` with pdflatex and bibtex on PATH. Dependencies: standard article, geometry, T1 fontenc/lmodern, amsmath/amssymb, booktabs,array,graphicx,url,natbib,hyperref and plainnat.bst. No IEEE class/logo/template is used or bundled. The editable single-file manuscript and all referenced figure/table exports are local relative paths. Figure regeneration is optional via figure_source/README.md and requires the supplied evidence Python dependencies. No journal or online submission was made. SHA256SUMS covers every shipped source payload; verify with the companion evidence scripts/verify_integrity.py and the source root argument.
