# Scientific contribution audit

The study audits a preserved implementation, not a newly invented controller. The experimental original and revised sources are identified in PROVENANCE.json and source/MANIFEST.json. Runtime assertions are separately labeled; historical data are retained. CLAIM_TO_IMPLEMENTATION_MATRIX.md provides algorithm, information, actions, objective, comparison and status for each contribution.

A digital twin here is a structured synthetic state plus analytical predictor. There is no physical synchronization, training, calibration-error model, measurement stream or learned state estimator. Network selection uses categorical coefficient multipliers. Placement uses cloud/edge/local coefficients and positive-capacity selection flags. Resource allocation is uniform, priority-based or approximately uniform SLSQP. Procedural adaptation is threshold-based and ignores its prior action when choosing. Revised service fidelity is a utility/latency/energy action, not prediction accuracy. Checkpoint state is causal synthetic episode state; no application checkpoint is executed.

Supported knowledge is narrow: the implementation's controller behavior differs from several labels; the full linear selector reduces to edge plus available minimum-penalty path; the exact symmetric share optimum is uniform, and its source solver only approximates feasibility; preserved procedural/adaptive outcomes are unfavorable, and transition charges reverse a small gross benefit. There is no demonstrated novel globally optimal controller, fair twin-information effect, true radio allocation gain, battery benefit or measured service continuity.

Methodological contribution is the traceable source-to-model-to-result audit, paired uncertainty and failure interpretation. Statistical reproducibility and honest negative findings can support an exploratory case study without establishing journal-level originality. Exact non-timing parity demonstrates fidelity to code, not realism of its assumptions. New source assertions reconstruct only historical inputs and do not add primary outcome samples. See SCIENTIFIC_READINESS_DECISION.md for framing and FIVE_REVIEWER_OBJECTIONS.md for significance limits.
