Missing SciPy or unsuccessful status falls back to uniform shares. The current focused checks required the slsqp backend; prior reproduction records also used it.

The exact solution in the frozen B20,N4..8 domain is uniform full-budget allocation; convex/KKT reasoning is in MATHEMATICAL_CHARACTERIZATION.md. This objective is not g,U,network selection,placement selection,energy depletion or a multi-slot switching objective. Equalizing shares optimizes this mathematical subproblem; it does not establish a useful radio-allocation controller because the scored primary metric depends on share sum, not user-specific delivered tasks/throughput.

This audit does not mark strict numerical feasibility or exact optimality true, and no existing frozen test was weakened or source changed.

Approximate solver behavior is sufficient for this numerical characterization, not for a physical resource guarantee. Other environments must check returned constraints/backend rather than assume bit identity. New engineering could require projection/residual rejection and a versioned protocol; it cannot retroactively replace existing results.
