\section{Limitations}
Results are \texttt{SYNTHETIC\_SIM} under a configured analytical metric, not operator KPIs and not SpectrumX judged IQ.
No sub-millisecond RF inference claim: compute columns are \texttt{HOST\_PROCESS\_TIMING}.
28~GHz in the ReadyGary companion is FR2~mmWave~\cite{3gpp_ts38101_2}, never Sub-6.
The model-oracle is hindsight-free only relative to the current-slot analytical metric, not relative to nature.
Cohen's $d$ uses five seed-level means of a low-noise simulator and can be large for small absolute $U$ gaps; interpret $d$ with the CIs.
Independent reproduction by an outside lab is still pending.
