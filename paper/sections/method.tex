\section{Method}
Protocol \texttt{paper/artifacts/experiment\_protocol.yaml} was frozen and committed before held-out JSON was generated.
Experiment \texttt{rq2\_cross\_layer\_continuity} is \texttt{SYNTHETIC\_SIM}: seeded episode draws of latency, loss, outage, energy, blockage, and compute, scored by the configured Gate~2 analytical metric (not field KPIs, not competition IQ).

\paragraph{Baselines.}
\texttt{no\_adaptation}, \texttt{static} (first-slot rule freeze), \texttt{local\_only}, \texttt{cloud\_only}, \texttt{edge\_only}, \texttt{rule\_based} (service-priority + outage pick), \texttt{optimization\_based} (SLSQP on $\sum\log(1+x_i)$ when SciPy is present), \texttt{twin\_informed}, \texttt{information\_equivalent} (same slot feature vector, frozen linear action scores, uniform shares), and a current-slot model-\texttt{oracle} over discrete placement$\times$network including switch penalties.

\paragraph{Splits.}
Train seeds $\{0,1,2,3,4\}$; held-out seeds $\{100,\ldots,104\}$; 24 episodes $\times$ 16 slots.
Domain-shift families: \texttt{high\_blockage}, \texttt{energy\_constrained}, \texttt{outage\_heavy}.
Ablations include zeroing edge observations, twin context, continuity class, energy, local-edge, and switch penalties.
Statistics: Student-$t$ 95\% CI and Cohen's $d$ versus \texttt{no\_adaptation}, aggregated seed-mean then CI (five seed means; df$=4$, $t_{0.975}=2.776$).
Compute time is \texttt{HOST\_PROCESS\_TIMING}.

\paragraph{ReadyGary companion.}
Sibling experiment \texttt{rq2\_beam\_selection\_fr2} uses a seeded ULA TDL generator at $f_c=28\times10^9$~Hz (FR2, not Sub-6)~\cite{3gpp_ts38101_2}.
The generator is not claimed as 3GPP TR~38.901-compliant.
DeepMIMO is related prior art for ray datasets~\cite{alkhateeb2019deepmimo} and was not executed for these tables.
