\subsection{Held-out orchestration}
\input{tables/rq2_heldout_policies}

On held-out seeds $\{100,\ldots,104\}$, mean service-continuity utility $U$ (net of switch penalties) was:
\texttt{information\_equivalent} $0.8185$ $[0.8135,0.8235]$,
model-\texttt{oracle} $0.8185$ $[0.8140,0.8231]$,
\texttt{static} $0.8160$ $[0.8129,0.8191]$,
\texttt{edge\_only} $0.8143$ $[0.8105,0.8182]$,
\texttt{no\_adaptation} $0.7965$ $[0.7910,0.8020]$,
\texttt{optimization\_based} $0.7965$ $[0.7910,0.8020]$ (Cohen's $d=0.0000$ versus \texttt{no\_adaptation} on $U$),
\texttt{rule\_based} $0.7941$ $[0.7885,0.7997]$,
\texttt{twin\_informed} $0.7840$ $[0.7805,0.7874]$ ($d=-3.4054$),
\texttt{cloud\_only} $0.7715$ $[0.7627,0.7802]$,
\texttt{local\_only} $0.6759$ $[0.6692,0.6826]$.
\texttt{information\_equivalent} and \texttt{oracle} CIs overlap; they are not distinguishable on this split.
Hypothesis H1 (twin-informed beats no-adaptation on held-out $U$) is \textbf{not} supported.
H2 is supported as a policy-structure effect: the same features with linear scoring ($U=0.8185$) outperformed the procedural+SLSQP twin policy ($U=0.7840$).
\texttt{adaptive\_checkpoint} paid the highest mean switch cost ($1.2459$); \texttt{twin\_informed} was $0.4833$ versus $0.1650$ for \texttt{no\_adaptation}.
SLSQP episode-sum compute exceeded the linear policy on this run; both are \texttt{HOST\_PROCESS\_TIMING}, not RF.

\subsection{Switching-cost ablation}
\input{tables/rq2_ablations}

Zeroing switch penalties raised held-out \texttt{twin\_informed} $U$ from $0.7840$ $[0.7805,0.7874]$ to $0.8142$ $[0.8104,0.8180]$ (H3 supported on this model).
\texttt{no\_edge\_observations} reports $U=0.9798$ because the ablation zeros latency and loss, which makes the analytical metric optimistic; it is not a field improvement.
Other ablations stayed near the full twin policy ($0.7840$).

\subsection{Domain shift}
\input{tables/rq2_domain_shift}

Under \texttt{outage\_heavy}, \texttt{twin\_informed} $U=0.6920$ exceeded \texttt{no\_adaptation} $0.6561$ (twin routing helps when terrestrial outage is frequent).
Under \texttt{high\_blockage}, \texttt{information\_equivalent} $0.6176$ led the listed non-oracle policies.
\texttt{energy\_constrained} ranked like the in-distribution held-out split.

\subsection{ReadyGary FR2 companion}
See the sibling repo tables (\texttt{rq2\_beam\_selection\_fr2}).
On held-out FR2 TDL draws, codebook \texttt{exhaustive\_oracle} mean SNR was $2.5263$~dB $[1.7954,3.2572]$, matching \texttt{cloud\_only} (same search).
Hierarchical \texttt{edge\_only} was $1.3182$~dB $[0.5571,2.0792]$ ($1.2082$~dB loss versus oracle).
Compute in that sibling table is \texttt{HOST\_PROCESS\_TIMING}, not sub-millisecond RF inference.
\texttt{twin\_informed} AoA-windowed search was $-3.6178$~dB versus \texttt{information\_equivalent} nearest-bin $-21.3758$~dB (same AoA hint; policy structure).
Removing the AoA hint made \texttt{twin\_informed} fall back to hierarchical search ($1.3182$~dB), which is better than the hinted window on this generator --- the AoA-to-DFT-bin map is a weak geometry proxy here, not a measured-RF beam tracker.
Sub-millisecond RF inference remains unproven.
