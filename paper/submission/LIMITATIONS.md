# Limitations

Five seed means per family limit inference. Priors and analytical score are uncalibrated. Original common Slot access does not equal feature consumption or computation. Static actions can remain unavailable and still score; capacity is a selector hint rather than workload feasibility. Some ablations alter scoring truth. Energy is a proxy without causal battery state. The oracle is greedy and lacks future/global optimality. Switch penalties are dimensionless configured charges. Fixed-trace cost rescaling is retrospective algebra and cannot establish reoptimized controller behavior. Revised and original outputs are separate vintages. No RF, carrier, satellite, deployed controller or physical device experiment validates these findings. Novelty, human verification, rights and declarations remain pending.
