# Owner scientific and editorial review worksheet

Prepared October 13, 2026. All fields below are pending; this is a review instrument, not evidence of completed approval.

1. Read the entire compiled manuscript and companion. Record reviewer name, date, reviewed commit SHA and explicit decision.
2. Recalculate the principal result using the supplement. Check whether the experimental unit, uncertainty, negative findings and nonclaims are explained accurately.
3. Review source-level limitations and decide whether this bounded methodological contribution meets the intended journal's originality threshold. A compile/test pass does not answer that question.
4. Inspect every figure and table, including units, labels, confidence interval meaning and correspondence to the retained JSON. Verify rights for software, template assets and manuscript content.
5. Confirm the author identity, ORCID, email, additional authors and contribution statements. Supply funding, sponsor, conflicts and prior-dissemination statements truthfully.
6. Confirm AI-use disclosure, final title/abstract/keywords, venue requirements, cover letter and any supplementary-upload requirements.
7. Decide whether to approve a private revision, seek independent specialist review, approve a public archive, or authorize submission. No choice is assumed in advance.
