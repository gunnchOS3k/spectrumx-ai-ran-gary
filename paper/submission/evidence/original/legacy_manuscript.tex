\begin{abstract}
Paper~II asks whether radio-aware digital-twin context plus simple policies (before learning) improves service continuity versus transparent baselines~\cite{itur_m2160,3gpp_ts22261}.
On held-out synthetic orchestration episodes, a frozen linear \texttt{information\_equivalent} policy matched the model-oracle and beat \texttt{no\_adaptation}; the procedural \texttt{twin\_informed} policy did not, because switching cost dominated the net utility.
ReadyGary companion draws at 28~GHz are FR2~mmWave~\cite{3gpp_ts38101_2}, not Sub-6; DeepMIMO is cited as related synthetic-ray methodology~\cite{alkhateeb2019deepmimo} and was not required for these runs.
Host-process timers are not RF latency.
\end{abstract}
